Welcome to SAR

The Saudi Railway Company (SAR) was established in May 2006 by a Cabinet decision and is wholly owned by the Public Investment Fund. In 2016, it was entrusted with the ownership and operation of all railway infrastructure projects between the Kingdom's cities.

Today, SAR is responsible for planning, building, and managing the railway network, from laying tracks and constructing bridges and tunnels, to operating passenger stations and dry ports, and providing integrated logistics solutions.

Whether transporting minerals and petrochemicals or serving pilgrims and travelers via high-speed trains, SAR, with its extensive portfolio and high reliability, represents an engine of the national economy and a bridge uniting the Kingdom's communities.

2030 Vision

At Saudi Arabia Railways, we are shaping the future of transportation through advanced training programs that empower Saudi talent, build industry-leading expertise, and support Saudi Vision 2030 toward a smarter, more sustainable future.

Strategic Objectives

  • Financial: Higher shareholder value and reduced reliance on government support, aiming to make SAR a profitable company by 2025.
  • Local Impact: Supporting local businesses and increasing local content.
  • Safety & Environment: Reducing accidents and carbon emissions.
  • Customer Service: Providing exceptional service that satisfies our customers.
  • Human Capital: Attracting and retaining highly qualified and experienced professionals in the Kingdom.
  • Infrastructure: Developing the railway infrastructure to be the best in Saudi Arabia.
